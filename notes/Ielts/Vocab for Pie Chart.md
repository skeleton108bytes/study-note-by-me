### Two percentages in one sentence

**number + percent**

- Energy production in the UK ==comprised/ made up of== 4% oil, 15% renewables, 22.5% nuclear power, 28.4% coal and 30.1% gas in 2014.
- In 2014, coal and gas ==accounted for 28.4% and 30.1%== of energy production in the UK, ==respectively.==
- In 2014, coal and gas ==accounted for 58.6%== of energy production in the UK, ==collectively.==
- The ==percentage== of energy produced oil was 4% in the UK in 2014.
- The chart illustrates== the percentage== of eletricity generated by fuel type in the UK.

### How to use percentages

Energy produced by  ___   accounted for a __ of the total energy in 2014 
- small/ large + percentage/ fraction/ segment


Energy produced by  ___   accounted for a __ of the total energy in 2014 **than** ___
- smaller/ larger + percentage/ fraction/ segment
